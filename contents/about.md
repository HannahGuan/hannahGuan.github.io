Hi! I am a MS student in Symbolic Systems at Stanford University. I'm interested in studying how do people and language models represent and update their understanding of others through communication, and how does this understanding guide belief revision and coordinated action

At Stanford, I have had the privilege of working on projects with [Prof. Jiaxin Pei](https://jiaxin-pei.github.io/) and [Prof. Robert Hawkins](https://rdhawkins.com/). I also contributed to a project on ontology led by [Prof. Tanya Luhrmann](https://www.tanyaluhrmann.com/), conducting fieldwork in China to examine people’s beliefs about different kinds of entities.

Before graduate school, I studied computer science and cognitive science at University of Michigan, where I was fortunate to work with Professors [Eytan Adar](https://www.cond.org/), [Michael Nebeling](https://www.michael-nebeling.de/), and [Lionel Robert](https://sites.google.com/umich.edu/lionelrobert/home).

Outside of research, I love indoor climbing, pickleball, anime, meditation, matcha, and spending time with my two cats.
