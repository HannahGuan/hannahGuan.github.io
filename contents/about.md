I am a first-year MS student in Symbolic Systems at Stanford University. I'm interested in studying how agents (human and artificial) establish, validate, and switch between one another's mental models to coordinate a shared understanding under conditions of uncertainty and information asymmetry.

Before graduate school, I studied computer science and cognitive science at University of Michigan, where I worked with Professors Eytan Adar, Michael Nebeling, and Lionel Robert. I also used to intern as an AI Product Manager.
